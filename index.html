The Continuum

Hi, I'm Josie Koenigs, a final-year Interior Design student at Georgian College. For my thesis I'm designing The Continuum, a community centre in the Barrie–Springwater area for neurodivergent adults.

Most buildings are designed without asking autistic and neurodivergent people what works for them. I don't want to guess. These questions are about spaces, words, school, community and the move into adulthood, and your answers will directly shape the design.

There are no right answers. Short answers, lists and "I don't know" are all useful.
